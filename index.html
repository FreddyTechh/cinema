ABOUT US

Freddy's Cinemas is the leading cinema chain of West Africa with 10 locations, 25 screens and over 2,700 seats in operation across Nigeria. FC is part of the Freddy's Group, a Nigerian based conglomerate established in 2022 with its key focus on hospitality and entertainment with diverse interests in Restaurants, Hotels, Industrial Catering, Cinemas, Family Entertainment Centres and Facility Management.
